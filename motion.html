Cosmic Motion

Watch the Solar System move, then zoom your imagination outward to the Milky Way and Andromeda. The animation is an educational visualization, not a precision ephemeris. Space is inconveniently enormous.

Keyboard: Space = pause/play · ←/→ = change speed · Home = reset · F = toggle labels

Scene 01

Planetary Motion

Normalized orbital paths showing the eight planets moving around the Sun. Orbital spacing, sizes and speeds are deliberately adjusted so the whole system fits on screen.

Speed × 50
Model basis: NASA describes the Solar System as a star-centered planetary system. This display uses scaled educational timing so the motion can be seen on screen. NASA planetary systems reference ↗.
Scene 02

Galactic Neighbors in Motion

A conceptual visualization of the Milky Way, Andromeda and Triangulum in the Local Group. The actual galaxy merger evolves over billions of years.

Conceptual scale
NASA has used Hubble measurements and simulations to model a future Milky Way–Andromeda encounter. This animation is an original educational visualization inspired by those findings, not a NASA simulation.
